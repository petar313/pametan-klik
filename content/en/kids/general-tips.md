**PROTECT YOURSELF**

- **Protect your privacy on the Internet**. This does not mean you have to give false information about yourself. Use your first name or nickname, not your full name.
- There could be some malicious people on the Internet who PRESENT THEMSELVES FALSLY. Keep away from them!
- NEVER EVER disclose your password of email accounts, profiles on social networking sites and chat sites.
- Use " strong " passwords that cannot be easily " broken" (a combination of uppercase and lowercase characters and numbers)
- Keep in mind that everything you write and publish on the Internet becomes public and accessible to a large number of people and you can no longer control how others use your data.

**PROTECT YOUR COMPUTER**

- Always install antivirus protection program on your computer and make sure it is always updated. If you don`t know how to do it, ask an adult to help you.
- Never surf the Internet if your computer is not protected by antivirus software.
- **Never click on pop-ups**. The safest is to close it by clicking the X in the upper right corner.You never know what the program will do, even if you click on the "No".
- Never download from the Internet without your parents` consent. Some sites contain viruses that may damage your computer.
- Always scan documents attached to e-mails with antivirus program before you open them.
- Do not open e-mails that you receive from people you don't know. **Delete them**!
- If you suspect that your computer is infected with a virus, tell this to your parents.
