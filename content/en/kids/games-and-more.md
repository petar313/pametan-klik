**GAMES**

- Do not download games from unknown web sites, you can infect your computer with viruses.
- When playing games on the Internet with unknown persons, do not reveal your personal data.

**WEB CAMERA**

- Communicate over the camera only with people you know and trust.

**Be SMART and popular, make a FRIENDLY CLICK**

- Do not take, do not publish or send photos and video clips that can embarrass someone or expose him/her to ridicule.
- Before you write a message, story or publish a photo, think whether you'll hurt anyone by this. Do not do to others what you would not like others to do to you.
- Do not send or forward messages that offend and disturb others. Ask yourself how would you feel if you received that message.
- Do not engage in discussions and chats that gossip or make fun of a person. It is a form of violence.
- If someone sends you threatening or malicious messages, do not reply. Show it to an adult whom you trust.
- Help your friends that are being harassed by someone over the Internet, do not hide such violence and immediately inform adults about what is going on.
