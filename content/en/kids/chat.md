key: kids-chat
title: Chat
description: How to chat safely online.
---

- Use only chat services that are designed for young people, preferably those where someone supervises the content in the chat rooms.
- **Never** give information about yourself, your family or friends to people you don't know. Don't reveal your surname, your address or which school you go to, because you don't know what their intentions are.
- **Never** send your photos, or pictures of your family or friends, to people you don't know, since they can be misused in many ways.
- Keep in mind that "unknown" people you chat with may present themselves falsely, give false information about themselves, or send someone else's photo.
- Don't write anything you would be ashamed of if your friends at school or in the neighbourhood read it. Anything you send could be used by a malicious person to blackmail you.
- If someone sends you harassing, threatening or obscene messages, stop chatting and be sure to tell your parents or another adult you trust. It is not your fault that someone threatens or insults you.
- Learn how to block people who send you unwanted messages.
- **Never** go alone to meet someone you met in a chat. If you want to meet an online friend in person, meet in a public place and bring friends or an adult you trust.
