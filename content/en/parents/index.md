---
key: parents
title: For parents
description: Tips for parents on keeping children safe online.
---

Today's children are the first generation to grow up with the Internet as an integral part of their lives. They use modern technology effortlessly, and are often far ahead of their parents in exploring online.

Although this difference may be uncomfortable, parents need to be involved in this virtual world, because children need guidance, advice and support from adults.

**Remember:** the opportunities and benefits of the Internet exceed its risks. Encourage your children to use it smartly and responsibly, and to explore all its positive opportunities.
